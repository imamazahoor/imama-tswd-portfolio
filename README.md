# About me
Hello! I'm currently a graduate student at Carnegie Mellon's Heinz College, pursuing an MS in Health Care Analytics & Information Technology (May 2027). I enjoy using data and machine learning to tackle complex challenges at the intersection of health, society, and the environment. 

In my free time, I enjoy reading books (classics and atmospheric horror are my favourite genres!) and trying new hobbies.

# What I hope to learn
I'm very excited about this class since effective data visualization and communication is an important skill and one I hope to improve over the course of the next few weeks. Here are some things I want to learn from this course:

1. Expanding my vocabulary for design critiques and articulating *why* I don't like a design better.
2. Tools to simplify my designs without sacrificing the primary message or insight.
3. Distinguishing between intention vs impact. What makes sense to me may not make sense to my audience.
